Create Your Own Pasta

Create Your Own Pasta starts at $13.99 for the plate. You pick a pasta and a sauce from the list, and both come included in the price, with soup or salad and two breadsticks on the side. Toppings cost extra, from $3.79 for broccoli to $6.79 for shrimp. The plate ranges from 530 to 1,300 calories based on what you pick.

Meal Combination

Meal combinations are the biggest trays on the menu. The Chicken Parmigiana Combination and the Lasagna Combination each serve 8 to 14 people at $224.00. Each tray pairs the main dish with sides for the group, so one order covers a full table. These trays suit office parties and family events where guests serve themselves.

Lighter Portion

If you read the olive garden menu with calories in mind, the light plates are easy to spot. Minestrone soup holds 110 calories a bowl, and the house salad without croutons matches it at 110 calories.
